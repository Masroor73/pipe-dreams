Pipe Dreams planner briefing. The agent started from version one. It tested 4 candidate revisions, C1 through C4, on validation data. A candidate had to win at least 2 of 3 origins and improve by at least 1 standard error. The revision gate accepted candidate C3, which became version two, the selected policy. On the final test, 2023 to 2025, version two caught 24.3 percent of future breaking assets on average across budgets, versus 18.2 percent for version one and 20.0 percent for a simple count-only baseline. 2,133 assets are escalated for human review. Limits: this is relative inspection priority, not failure prediction.
